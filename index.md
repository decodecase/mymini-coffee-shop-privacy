# My Mini Coffee Shop – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 October 2026_

My Mini Coffee Shop ("the game") is a virtual reality game for Meta Quest headsets, made by [] ("we", "us")OKAN CETINKAYA. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play the game, including the beta version.

**In short: the game does not collect, sell or share your personal information. Your cafés and settings stay on your headset.**

## 1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game has:

- no user accounts or sign-in of its own
- no advertising
- no analytics or tracking of its own
- no in-app purchases
- no chat, voice or multiplayer features

## 2. Information stored on your headset

The game saves the following only on your own headset, so you can continue where you left off:

- **Your saved cafés:** the café name you choose, day, money, stock, progress and similar game data.
- **Your settings:** language, units, currency, comfort (standing or seated, view height), sound volumes and help options.

This information is never sent to us. You can delete a café with the "Delete café" button on the opening menu. Uninstalling the game removes all of its data from your headset.

## 3. Your body, hands and surroundings

To let you play, the headset tracks the position of your head, hands and controllers. Meta's system software processes this on the device in real time. The game uses it only while you play. It does not record or store it, and does not send it anywhere.

The game does **not** use:

- the microphone
- the cameras or passthrough
- eye, face or body tracking

## 4. Third-party services

- **Meta Horizon platform:** the game is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store and may ask the platform to confirm that you are entitled to play it, for example that you joined the beta. Meta handles your Meta account and device data under its own privacy policy: https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/
- **Unity engine:** the game is built with Unity. The Unity engine may send anonymous technical information about the device, such as the headset model and operating system version, to Unity Technologies. This information does not identify you. See https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

We have no control over, and receive no personal data from, these services.

## 5. Beta feedback

If you choose to contact us, for example by email or on our community channels, we receive only what you send, such as your message, screenshots and username. We use it only to answer you and to improve the game. We do not share it, and we delete it on request.

## 6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Since the game collects no personal information, none is collected from children either.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding online features, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
